# Cucurucho — MVP User Test Checklist

Concise task list for a real usability session with a paid-media analyst. Each task should be attempted without guidance beyond what the product itself surfaces; note where the person hesitated, misclicked, or asked "what does this mean?"

1. **Upload a Meta or Google report** — drag a real exported CSV/XLSX into `/contribute`. Does the person notice the platform was recognized automatically, or do they expect to have to say which platform it is?
2. **Complete campaign context** — fill in Objective/Vertical/Country once for the whole file. Is it clear this applies to every row, not just one?
3. **Review before confirming** — look at the mapping/review screen. Can the person tell which columns were understood, which are ignored, and why, without asking?
4. **Confirm the import and view campaign readiness** — after import, check `/account/contributions` and open one campaign's detail. Can they tell which metrics were imported vs. calculated?
5. **Compare a just-imported campaign** — from the import success screen, use "Compare this campaign." Does the pre-filled context feel accurate, or confusing?
6. **Run a benchmark from scratch** — use the homepage discovery flow to find a benchmark without importing anything first. Do they understand what "cohort" means from the UI alone?
7. **Save a comparison, then find it again** — save a benchmark result, navigate away, and return to the homepage. Do they find "Continue working" on their own?
8. **Explore the media catalog** — go to `/platforms`, open a media profile. Is it clear what "data completeness" means for that outlet?
9. **Build a media plan** — select 2–4 media opportunities and reach a budget scenario summary. Does the comparability warning (when shown) make sense?
10. **Return to a saved plan** — leave `/planner`, come back later, reopen the saved scenario. Do the numbers matching (or differing) from before raise unexpected questions?
11. **Notice a data gap** — on the homepage workspace, does the person understand what a "you could add more" message is asking them to do, and do they know where to do it?
12. **Find their own contributions again** — without being told, can they locate their own imported campaigns a few days later?

Keep this list under 12 tasks and re-run it after any change to onboarding, import, or navigation — not as a general QA pass.
